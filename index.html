About Me

From Bedside to Virtual Care

As a Registered Nurse, I've had the opportunity to care for patients in different clinical settings, and those experiences have strengthened not only my clinical skills but also my ability to communicate, stay organized, and remain calm under pressure. I genuinely enjoy helping people and finding practical solutions that make healthcare run more smoothly.

Over time, I've developed a strong interest in the administrative side of healthcare. I'm skilled in clinical documentation, patient communication, appointment scheduling, electronic medical records, and coordinating care while maintaining confidentiality and professionalism. I'm also comfortable learning and working with new healthcare technologies.

I believe that reliability, attention to detail, and empathy are just as important as technical skills. Whether I'm supporting patients directly or helping a healthcare team behind the scenes, I take pride in delivering quality work and creating a positive experience for everyone involved.

I'm currently open to opportunities in Medical Virtual Assistance, healthcare administration, care coordination, and other remote healthcare support roles where I can continue to grow while making a meaningful impact.
